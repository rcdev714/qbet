## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How do I get beta access?

Anymarkt is currently in a **private beta** for Ecuador (`EC` launch jurisdiction).

1. Visit **Request access** on the landing page or go to `/request-access`
2. Submit your email and confirm Ecuador residence
3. Our team reviews requests in the admin queue
4. When approved, you receive an email with a link to `/beta/welcome`
5. Sign up or sign in with the **same email** you used in the request form

If you were directly invited, use the email address that received the invite.

### I submitted a request but haven't heard back

- Check spam for mail from `@anymarkt.com`
- Ensure you signed up with the **same email** as your access request
- If already signed in, the app may show the beta waitlist — it refreshes automatically when approved
- Contact **support@anymarkt.com** with the email you used

### What is Play Mode?

Play Mode is a risk-free way to test your prediction skills. You receive virtual tokens to wager on markets. These tokens have **no real-world value**.

### Can I withdraw Play Mode tokens?

No. Play Mode tokens are for simulation and social ranking only. They cannot be exchanged for money.

### How do I verify my identity for real money?

Go to **Wallet → Verify** (`/wallet/verify`) and complete Stripe Identity verification. Live wallet features unlock after successful KYC.

### How do I delete my account?

Delete your account from **Profile → Settings** in the app.

### Which countries are supported?

During the EC beta launch, **Ecuador** is the primary supported residence country. Other countries may map to the US compliance framework — see in-app residence picker.

### I need to change my country of residence

Country of residence is locked after onboarding because payment accounts bind to the country supplied at signup. Email **support@anymarkt.com** for manual review.
